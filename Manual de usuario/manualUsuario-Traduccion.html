Candidate Features

Login

This section is the gateway to your professional profile. To ensure the security of your information and provide a personalized experience, you must identify yourself within our system.

Credential Update

Within your profile settings, you have the option to modify your access key at any time. This feature is ideal if you wish to update your security or simply change your current key to one that is easier for you to remember.

Forgot Your Password?

To ensure the security and accessibility of your professional profile, the system allows you to update your password from the settings as long as you know your current key and wish to replace it with one that is easier to remember; however, in the event that you have completely forgotten your credentials, you must complete a Google Form to verify your identity, allowing the administrator to validate your request, perform an internal reset, and send you the new access credentials via email so you can resume your activity immediately.

Professional Profile

Create and manage your profile. Upload your resume, add work experience, and academic background to stand out to companies.

Documents and Resume

Manage all your professional documents in one place. Upload different versions of your resume, cover letters, certificates, and other relevant documents. Easily attach them to your applications and keep them always accessible.

Administrator Features

Job Search and Filters

Use the advanced search tools to find job offers that match your profile. Filter by sector, location, contract type, salary, and required experience. Save your searches to receive automatic notifications when new matching offers are published.
